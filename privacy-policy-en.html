Privacy Policy

Anime Block Quest - Last updated: January 11, 2026

Summary: We respect your privacy. We only collect data necessary for game functionality and ad display. We do not sell your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

Anime Block Quest may collect the following information:

  • Game Data: Score, progress, achievements, and game preferences (stored locally on device)
  • Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID) for personalized ad display
  • Login Data (optional): If you choose to sign in with Google, we collect name and email for progress synchronization
  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with the app to improve the experience

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Save and sync your game progress
  • Display ads through Google AdMob
  • Maintain rankings and leaderboards
  • Improve user experience
  • Provide technical support

3. Advertising

We use Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may collect:

  • Device identifiers
  • Approximate location data (IP-based)
  • Usage data for ad personalization

You can opt out of personalized ads in your Android device settings: Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization.

For more information: Google Privacy Policy

4. Data Storage

Your game data is stored:

  • Locally: On your device storage (AsyncStorage)
  • In the cloud (optional): If you sign in with Google, your data may be synced with Firebase

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties, except:

  • Advertising service providers (Google AdMob)
  • Analytics providers (Firebase Analytics)
  • When required by law

6. Security

We implement technical and organizational security measures to protect your data, including encryption and secure development practices.

7. User Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction of incorrect data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent for data collection
  • Export your data

To exercise these rights, please contact us.

8. Children

Anime Block Quest is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent and believe your child has provided us with data, please contact us.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or other means.
