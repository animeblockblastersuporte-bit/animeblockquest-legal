Delete Account and Data

Anime Block Quest - How to request deletion of your data

Summary: You can delete your account and all associated data directly in the app or by email.

1. How to Delete Your Data

Option 1: In-App (Recommended)

You can delete your account and all data directly in the app:

Settings → Delete My Account

This will immediately remove all your data from our servers and log you out.

Option 2: Email Request

If you cannot access the app, send an email to:

animeblockblaster.suporte@gmail.com

Include the email address associated with your Google account. We will process your request within 30 days.

2. Data That Will Be Deleted

  • Profile information: Name, email, Google photo
  • Game statistics: Scores, games played, lines cleared
  • Ranking position: Position and history in global ranking
Warning: Account deletion is permanent and irreversible. All data will be removed and cannot be recovered.

3. Guest Players

If you play as Guest without logging in, your data is stored only on your device. Simply uninstall the app to remove all data.

4. Processing Time

Email requests will be processed within 30 days. You will receive a confirmation when the deletion is complete.

5. Contact

For questions about data deletion:

Email: animeblockblaster.suporte@gmail.com
